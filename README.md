## The Bird Flinches

> The bus's turn signal clicks not for the driver, but for a bird perched on a wire who flinches each time.

## Why it wins

The click is for the bird, not the driver: a non-human witness to human machinery turns a throwaway sound into a small, strange drama. The punch lands in the last words, "flinches each time", where the tiny repeated reaction makes the moment feel real and new.

`main.ts` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`) with no trailing newline.
