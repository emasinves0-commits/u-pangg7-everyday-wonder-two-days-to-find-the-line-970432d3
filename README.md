## The Hand Arrives First

> The escalator handrail moves a fraction faster than the steps, so my hand arrives at the top a little before I do.

## Why it wins

The wonder is a real, checkable mechanism, not a metaphor: an escalator's handrail genuinely drifts a fraction faster than its steps. The punch lands in the last words, "a little before I do", where your own hand becomes a separate traveller that beats you to your destination — the body outrunning itself, in one clean read.

`main.ts` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`) with no trailing newline.
